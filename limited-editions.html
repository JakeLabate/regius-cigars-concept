Limited editions

Cigars Regius made in small numbers, for particular shops, or only once. All are drawn to the same scale as the regular range.

2013, then yearly

Selección Orchant

Mitchell Orchant of C.Gars Ltd and Alfie Turmeaus Tobacconist is one of the largest sellers of Cuban cigars in Britain, and lends his name to the Havanas he selects. In 2013 he and Akhil Kapacee made a Regius to the same standard: a Robusto modelled on the Partagás Serie D No. 4, which Kapacee described to halfwheel as “classically Cuban”.

Four hundred boxes of ten were made, half for Britain and half for the United States. The cigar wears a second, golden band. It came back in 2014 with a new size, and C.Gars now lists the Orchant Selección as a yearly release blended for British palates.

Robusto, 2013. 4 3/4 in × 52. Nicaraguan wrapper, binder and filler. Plasencia Cigars S.A.

2013

The three-wrapper Culebra

A culebra is three slim cigars twisted together while still supple. Regius made this one for Alfie Turmeaus with a different wrapper on each cigar: a Cuban-seed claro, a Cuban-seed oscuro and the Talanga Connecticut.

Binder and filler are the same in all three, so the smoker can untwist them and taste exactly what a wrapper does. They were sold in boxes of three culebras, nine cigars in all.

5 3/4 in × 39 each. Boxes of three culebras.

2012

Winston's Humidor Third Anniversary Culebra

Winston's Humidor of Midlothian, Virginia, was one of the first American shops to stock Regius. For its third anniversary in 2012 Regius made it a culebra with a different wrapper on each cigar. halfwheel reviewed the Rosado and the Oscuro from it separately.

The culebra made for Turmeaus a year later has the same size and the same mix of wrappers. Kapacee has said the blend inside is different.

5 3/4 in × 39 each. Nicaraguan Jalapa Rosado wrapper on the Rosado. Plasencia Cigars S.A.

2014

Damaris

A very small release made with Jim Thomas of That Cigar Place. It wore a Mexican San Andrés wrapper two years before the Oscuro Especial made that leaf a regular part of the range.

No dimensions were published in the sources used here, so it is not drawn.

2015

Limited Edition Corona Gorda

An offshoot of Black Label in a Cuban-style box press. It wears the Black Label band, but Kapacee told Cigar Coop the blend is slightly different, and the cigars were aged for two years before release.

5 5/8 in × 46, box-pressed. Nicaraguan wrapper, binder and filler.

Limited production

Serie Limitada

The one Regius made entirely of Peruvian tobacco. Wrapper, binder and filler all come from Tabacalera del Oriente in Tarapoto, and the cigars are matured for more than a year. C.Gars, which announced the line, says the Peruvian leaf gives the cigar a layer of sweetness.

Made as a Corona, Petit Royales, Robusto and Wide Churchill. Dimensions were not confirmed, so none is drawn.

2012

White Label

The first Regius made outside Nicaragua. When SAG Imports took on American distribution in 2012, its sister factory MATASA in the Dominican Republic began rolling a Dominican puro under the Regius name. Cigar Aficionado gave the Royal Robusto 90 points in December 2013.

When Regius and Quesada parted in 2017, Quesada said it was no longer making the line. It should not be confused with the White Label of the Exclusivo U.S.A. series, which is Nicaraguan.

Royal Robusto 5 5/8 in × 54, Toro 6 in × 50, Torpedo 6 1/8 in × 52. Dominican wrapper, binder and filler.

2013

The £40,000 Double Corona

In 2013 a single boxed Regius Double Corona was sold at Alfie Turmeaus Tobacconist in Shepherd Market, Mayfair, for £40,000. The trade press reported it as the most expensive cigar ever sold, and it still appears on lists of the costliest cigars in the world.

The price bought more than the cigar. Regius flew the buyer to Nicaragua to see the factory, then blended and rolled a thousand cigars to his own taste in flavour, size and strength. Kapacee cut and lit the Double Corona himself.

7 5/8 in × 49. Handmade in Nicaragua.
