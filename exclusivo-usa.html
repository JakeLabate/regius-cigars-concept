Exclusivo U.S.A.

Three blends made for American smokers and sold only in the United States. Red, then White, then Blue.

Why America got its own

Regius had been built for British and European tastes. In 2014 Akhil Kapacee turned to the United States, telling Cigar Aficionado he wanted a line that kept traditional flavours but came in the modern, inventive sizes the American market asks for.

The series debuted that May at Casa de Montecristo and went national soon after, first in four sizes and then in six. Regius has said each blend is adjusted for each size, so a Robusto and a Lancero Extra of the same colour are not the same recipe cut to different lengths.

All three were rolled at Plasencia Cigars S.A. in Nicaragua.

The three blends

2014

Red Label

Exclusivo U.S.A.

A Nicaraguan puro with a wrapper grown in the Jalapa valley and filler that includes tobacco from the island of Ometepe. Cigar Aficionado tasted cocoa and earth with a nutty, pistachio edge in the Pressed Perfecto, gave it 92 points and ranked it no. 21 among its Top 25 cigars of 2017.

2014

White Label

Exclusivo U.S.A. Claro Especial

The second release, later the same year. It is also Nicaraguan throughout and is built on a double binder. Cigar Coop tasted cream, mixed fruit, natural tobacco and pepper across the sizes and placed it no. 22 in its 2014 Cigar of the Year Countdown.

Wrapper
Nicaragua
Binder
Nicaragua, double binder
Filler
Nicaragua

2016

Blue Label

Exclusivo U.S.A. Oscuro Especial

The last of the three, shipped in February 2016 and the strongest. Kapacee chose a dark Mexican San Andrés wrapper for its sweetness and clean finish, and five of the six sizes are box-pressed. Cigar Coop scored the Robusto 92 and placed it no. 27 in its 2016 Countdown.

Six sizes

The series shares one set of sizes across all three blends. Two are perfectos, tapered at both ends, and none of them is small. They are drawn here with the Red Label's wrapper.
