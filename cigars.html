The cigars

Six lines carry the Regius band. Sizes are given as length in inches by ring gauge, where one ring is 1/64 of an inch. Every drawing on this page is to one scale, so a Corona and a Gran Toro can be judged side by side.

Black Label

The original. Black Label reached British tobacconists in 2010 and is the cigar the rest of the range is measured against: a medium-bodied Nicaraguan puro, blended to give smokers of Cuban cigars something they would recognise.

A series of three blends made for the American market and sold only there. The Red Label and the White Label, or Claro Especial, arrived in 2014. The Blue Label, or Oscuro Especial, closed the series in February 2016. All three share six sizes, two of them perfectos.

Connecticut

Regius showed its Connecticut at the IPCPR trade show in July 2017. The wrapper is shade grown from Connecticut seed in the Talanga valley of Honduras, and sits over Nicaraguan binder and filler.

Most Connecticut-seed wrapper is grown in Ecuador. Regius chose Talanga leaf instead, and its own product notes say the choice was made for flavour.

Sun Grown

Shown beside the Connecticut in 2017, the Sun Grown sits at the other end of the range. It wears a dark sun grown wrapper taken from the higher primings of the plant, over Nicaraguan binder and filler, and it was made to be the fuller, bolder cigar in the range.

Maduro

The Regius Maduro went into wide release in March 2018, after a spell in which only a few retailers had it. Regius describes it as the complexity of Black Label with the blend adjusted to suit a rich maduro wrapper. The details of that blend have not been published.

Akhil Kapacee has traced the idea to Spanish jamón: if what a pig eats in its last weeks decides the ham, then what a tobacco plant is fed should decide the leaf.

Reserva

Reserva is made in small batches from Nicaraguan tobacco and aged after rolling. Kapacee has said these medium to medium-plus cigars were blended to recall the Cuban Bolívar of the years when it was most sought after.

The 2021 edition came as a Robusto Gordo in black lacquered boxes of twenty. British stockists have also carried a 50 ring Robusto in boxes of ten.
