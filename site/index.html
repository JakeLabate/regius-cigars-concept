Regius is a London cigar house, founded in 2010. Its cigars are rolled by hand in Estelí, Nicaragua, by the Plasencia family, and were first blended for smokers raised on Cuban cigars. The motto on the band asks one thing of whoever is holding it: be the judge of elegance.

Six lines, one band

All are handmade, long-filler cigars. The swatch beside each name shows the shade of its wrapper leaf.

  • Black Label

    The first Regius cigar, sold in Britain since 2010. A medium-bodied Nicaraguan puro in eight sizes.

  • Exclusivo U.S.A.

    Three blends made for American smokers and sold only in the United States: Red, White and Blue Label.

  • Connecticut

    A shade wrapper grown from Connecticut seed in the Talanga valley of Honduras, over Nicaraguan binder and filler.

  • Sun Grown

    A dark, higher-priming sun grown wrapper on an all-Nicaraguan blend, made as the fuller, bolder cigar in the range.

  • Maduro

    The complexity of Black Label reworked around a maduro wrapper. Five sizes, in wide release since March 2018.

  • Reserva

    Small batches of all-Nicaraguan tobacco, aged after rolling and blended to recall the Cuban Bolívar.

The name and the motto

Regius is Latin for royal: of a king. The motto on the band, arbiter elegantiae, means judge of elegance.

It was the standing of Gaius Petronius at the court of Nero. Petronius gave his life to pleasure, yet Rome remembered him as a connoisseur, and the emperor took his word as final on any question of taste. Regius was named in his memory.

The motto hands his office to the smoker. The cigar is made; the verdict is yours.

Rolled in Estelí

The core Regius blends are made at Plasencia Cigars S.A. in Estelí, in the mountains of northern Nicaragua. Regius describes its partners as cigar makers of Cuban descent with more than 500 rollers at the bench.

The tobacco is Nicaraguan, some of it aged for years before it is rolled. Regius says every cigar is checked by hand for draw and construction before it leaves the factory.
