The house

Regius Cigars Ltd is a London company. Its cigars are designed in England and rolled in Nicaragua, and its name and motto are Roman.

A Latin name

Regius means royal, or belonging to a king. The house uses it plainly: a hand-made king of cigars.

The motto on the band is arbiter elegantiae, judge of elegance. It belongs to Gaius Petronius, a Roman writer born around AD 27 and a courtier to the emperor Nero. Petronius devoted himself to pleasure, but he was known as a connoisseur and not a wastrel. Tacitus records that Nero came to think nothing charming or refined unless Petronius had approved it.

Regius was created in his memory, and the motto is an invitation. The makers cultivate, age and blend the tobacco for taste, an even burn and a good aroma. Whether they have succeeded is for the smoker to judge.

Founded in London

Regius was founded in 2010 by a small group of cigar enthusiasts in London. It is led by Akhil Kapacee, who was born in the city and worked as an investment banker at Barclays Capital before he made cigars.

Kapacee blended his first cigars for the European palate. In practice that meant people who mostly smoked Havanas, and the aim was a Nicaraguan cigar with a character they would recognise. The American market came later, and with it a second idea of what a Regius could be.

Made by the Plasencias

To make the cigars, the founders went to Nestor Plasencia, whose family has grown tobacco for five generations and whose roots are in Cuban cigar making. The core Regius blends are rolled at Plasencia Cigars S.A. in Estelí, in the mountains of northern Nicaragua.

Regius describes a factory of more than 500 rollers making cigars the old-fashioned way with modern technology behind them. Every cigar is checked individually for draw and construction before it leaves.

Two cigars were made elsewhere. The first White Label was rolled at MATASA in the Dominican Republic, and the Serie Limitada uses only Peruvian tobacco. Both are on the limited editions page.

Year by year

  1. 2009

    Regius Cigars Limited is incorporated in London on 22 July, under its first name, Trinity Cigars Limited.

  2. 2010

    The company takes the name Regius on 1 July. The first cigars reach British tobacconists.

  3. 2011

    Late in the year Regius is sold in the United States for the first time, through Sacred Cigars of Dallas. Three of its four sizes make the crossing.

  4. 2012

    SAG Imports, the distribution arm of Quesada Cigars, becomes the exclusive American distributor. The Dominican-made White Label follows, and Winston's Humidor in Virginia receives an anniversary culebra.

  5. 2013

    The Selección Orchant Robusto and a three-wrapper Culebra are made with Mitchell Orchant. A single Double Corona is sold in Mayfair for £40,000.

  6. 2014

    Exclusivo U.S.A. launches: the Red Label in May, the Claro Especial later in the year. Damaris is made in very small numbers.

  7. 2015

    A box-pressed Limited Edition Corona Gorda appears, aged for two years.

  8. 2016

    The Oscuro Especial ships in February and completes the Exclusivo U.S.A. series.

  9. 2017

    The agreement with Quesada ends on 30 April, and Oscar Valladares Tobacco & Co. of Houston takes over on 1 May. Connecticut and Sun Grown are shown at the IPCPR trade show in July. In December the Pressed Perfecto is named no. 21 in Cigar Aficionado's Top 25.

  10. 2018

    The Maduro goes into wide release in March.

  11. 2021

    The 2021 edition of Reserva arrives in black lacquered boxes.
